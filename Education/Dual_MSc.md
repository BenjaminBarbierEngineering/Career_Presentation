# Master's Degree – Imaging, Robotics and Engineering for Life Sciences (IRIV)

**Period:** 09/2021 - 08/2023  
**University / School:** Télécom Physique Strasbourg, University of Strasbourg  
**Location:** Pôle API, 300 Boulevard Sébastien Brant, 67400 Illkirch-Graffenstaden, France

## Program Overview

The Master's program in Imaging, Robotics and Engineering for Life Sciences (IRIV) was a research-oriented university degree pursued concurrently with the engineering curriculum.

This multidisciplinary program focused on advanced imaging technologies, image processing, robotics and their applications in scientific, industrial and biomedical fields. It aimed to develop expertise in the theoretical foundations and practical implementation of imaging systems, intelligent algorithms and robotic technologies.

The program combined mathematics, physics, computer science and engineering, with an emphasis on research, technological innovation and the development of advanced solutions for applications ranging from computer vision and image analysis to medical imaging and computer-assisted procedures.

## Major Subjects Covered

- Scientific and Medical Imaging
- Advanced Image Processing and Image Analysis
- Computer Vision and 3D Vision
- Digital Signal Processing
- Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence
- Deep Learning and Pattern Recognition
- Robotics and Automation
- Robotic Control and System Modelling
- Image-Guided Robotics and Computer-Assisted Procedures
- Biomedical Engineering and Medical Technologies
- Biomechanics and Biomedical Systems
- Medical Image Processing and Analysis
- Statistical Methods and Mathematical Modelling
- Research Methodology and Scientific Projects

## Qualification and Academic Level

- French National University Master's Degree (Diplôme National de Master)
- Master IRIV – Imaging, Robotics and Engineering for Life Sciences
- Master's-level qualification (Bac+5)
- Level 7 of the European Qualifications Framework (EQF)
- Research-oriented degree co-accredited by the University of Strasbourg and INSA Strasbourg.
- Designed to prepare graduates for R&D, scientific research and advanced engineering positions in imaging, computer vision, robotics, medical technologies and biomedical engineering.

## Skills Acquired

- **Advanced Imaging & Computer Vision:** Understanding and development of imaging systems, image processing techniques, computer vision algorithms and 3D imaging solutions.
- **Robotics & Automation:** Knowledge of robotic systems, motion control, automation and the integration of perception algorithms into robotic applications.
- **Artificial Intelligence & Image Analysis:** Application of machine learning and deep learning techniques to imaging and scientific data.
- **Biomedical Engineering:** Understanding of medical imaging technologies, biomedical systems and engineering applications in healthcare.
- **Scientific Research & Modelling:** Mathematical modelling, experimental approaches, scientific investigation and evaluation of advanced algorithms.
- **Multidisciplinary Engineering:** Ability to bridge computer science, applied mathematics, physics, robotics and biomedical technologies.
