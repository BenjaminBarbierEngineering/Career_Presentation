# Professional Bachelor's Degree – Avionics Systems Maintenance (MSA)


**Period:** 09/2018 - 08/2019  
**University / School:** University Institute of Technology (IUT) Montpellier-Sète, University of Montpellier  
**Location:** 99 Avenue d'Occitanie, 34090 Montpellier, France

## Program Overview

This career-oriented, one-year university program was designed to train specialists in aircraft maintenance, with a particular focus on avionics systems and onboard instrumentation.

The curriculum combined theoretical engineering knowledge with practical technical training in aircraft technologies, electronic systems, automatic control and aeronautical maintenance procedures. It aimed to develop the technical, organizational and operational skills required to manage, perform and supervise aircraft maintenance activities, including inspection, testing, fault diagnosis and quality assurance.

Delivered exclusively through a work-study apprenticeship model, the program combined academic instruction with extensive professional experience in an industrial aerospace environment, enabling students to apply engineering principles directly to real-world aircraft maintenance operations.

## Major Subjects Covered

- Aircraft Avionics Systems and Onboard Instrumentation
- Aircraft Electrical and Electronic Systems
- Electronics and Embedded Systems
- Automatic Control and Aircraft System Control
- Aircraft Navigation and Communication Systems
- Aircraft Maintenance Methods and Procedures
- Aircraft Maintenance Regulations (EASA Part-145, Part-M)
- ATA Specification and Aircraft Technical Documentation
- Aircraft Systems Architecture and Technology
- Aerodynamics and Flight Mechanics
- Aircraft Propulsion Systems
- Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) Methods
- Fault Diagnosis, Inspection and Troubleshooting
- Aircraft Maintenance Organization and Quality Procedures
- Technical Reporting and Professional Communication
- Technical English

## Qualification and Academic Level

- French National Professional Bachelor's Degree (*Licence Professionnelle*)
- Official qualification: *Métiers de l'industrie – Industrie aéronautique*, specialization in *Maintenance des Systèmes Avioniques (MSA)*
- Bachelor's Degree (Bac+3)
- Level 6 of the European Qualifications Framework (EQF)
- 60 European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS) credits
- Nationally recognized university qualification awarded by the University of Montpellier
- Profession-oriented qualification designed to prepare graduates for technical and supervisory positions in aircraft maintenance, avionics testing, quality assurance and aerospace operations.

## Skills Acquired

- **Avionics & Aircraft Systems:** Understanding of aircraft electronic architectures, onboard instrumentation, avionics systems and aircraft electrical systems.
- **Electronics & Automatic Control:** Knowledge of electronic circuits, embedded systems, automatic control principles and aircraft system operation.
- **Aircraft Maintenance & Troubleshooting:** Application of maintenance methodologies, technical inspections, fault diagnosis, testing and corrective maintenance procedures.
- **Aerospace Regulations & Airworthiness:** Understanding of aircraft maintenance regulations, EASA requirements, technical documentation and aviation safety standards.
- **Testing & Quality Assurance:** Execution of technical checks, interpretation of test results, inspection procedures and maintenance quality control.
- **Technical Documentation & Communication:** Preparation of technical reports, maintenance documentation and communication in an international aerospace environment.
- **Industrial Operations & Teamwork:** Organization of maintenance activities, coordination with technical teams and application of engineering knowledge in operational environments.

## Projects, Experience, and Achievements

The program was completed through a professional apprenticeship, combining university education with hands-on experience in the aerospace industry.

This work-study structure provided direct exposure to real-world aircraft maintenance operations, allowing the practical application of knowledge in avionics, electronics, aircraft systems and maintenance procedures. It also developed an understanding of industrial constraints, technical documentation, safety requirements and quality standards within a professional aviation environment.
