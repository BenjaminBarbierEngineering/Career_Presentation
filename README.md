# Benjamin Barbier

**R&D Engineer — Autonomous Systems, Perception and Localization**

I am an R&D engineer specializing in real-time perception and localization systems for safety-critical transportation applications. My work combines advanced mathematics, 3D geometry, numerical optimization, computer vision and modern C++ software engineering to deliver robust algorithms for complex, dynamic environments.

At **Alstom Transport**, I contribute to the development of perception and decision-support solutions for autonomous trains and railway Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS). I design and implement LiDAR-based obstacle and rail detection, GNSS and digital-map integration, railway-localization algorithms and deterministic trajectory modeling using clothoids and track geometry. I also develop real-time software architectures and automated experimentation tools for large-scale validation.

My work has contributed to a complete perception and decision-support system deployed on a tram operating in commercial urban service in Zurich. More than 100 functional and operational scenarios have been validated on test benches and in the field. Rail-aware filtering and contextualized Areas of Interest have significantly reduced obstacle-detection false positives while maintaining real-time performance.

My experience also includes applied artificial intelligence and industrial engineering. At **Vinci Facilities**, I developed reinforcement-learning algorithms and building digital twins to optimize HVAC control and heat-pump setpoints for energy-efficient operation. Earlier, at **Chromalloy**, I led an aerospace manufacturing improvement project that introduced an optimized machining strategy, projected annual savings of approximately €12,000 and reduced annual operator workload by 135 hours. At **Sabena Technics**, I worked on avionics maintenance for military and civilian aircraft, strengthening my understanding of diagnostics, reliability, quality assurance and aerospace operating standards.

I hold a French Engineering Degree equivalent to a Master of Science, with a specialization in Images, Signals and Data Science. I also hold a research-oriented Master's degree in Imaging, Robotics and Engineering for Life Sciences. This multidisciplinary background gives me a strong foundation across signal processing, computer vision, machine learning, robotics, data science and system integration.
