# Volunteer Firefighter

**Period:** May 2015 - August 2023  
**Locations:**
- **CS Beaucaire** (until April 2022): 235 chemin de la Tapie, 30300 Beaucaire, France
- **SDIS 67 CSP Sud:** 33 route de Neuhof, 67400 Illkirch-Graffenstaden, France

## Context

Served as a volunteer firefighter in Beaucaire until April 2022, then in Strasbourg through August 2023.

## Training and qualifications

- First aid, CPR, and pre-hospital emergency medical care.
- Road traffic accident response, including vehicle access and extrication.
- Structural firefighting, including interior firefighting and ladder operations.
- Wildland firefighting specialization.
- Team leader (binôme) training for firefighting interventions.

## Skills developed

- Operational rigor.
- Team management and communication.
- Risk analysis.
