# Master of Science in Engineering – Images, Signals and Data Science (ISSD)

**Period:** 09/2020 - 08/2023  
**University / School:** Télécom Physique Strasbourg, University of Strasbourg  
**Location:** Pôle API, 300 Boulevard Sébastien Brant, 67400 Illkirch-Graffenstaden, France

## Program Overview

This three-year engineering program provided multidisciplinary training in engineering sciences, with a final-year specialization in Images, Signals and Data Science (ISSD). The specialization focused on developing advanced expertise in the acquisition, processing, analysis and interpretation of complex signals, images and data.

The curriculum combined strong theoretical foundations in mathematics, signal processing and statistical methods with practical engineering applications in computer vision, machine learning and artificial intelligence. It aimed to prepare engineers to design, develop and implement advanced algorithms and intelligent systems for industrial, scientific and technological applications.

## Major Subjects Covered

- Digital Signal Processing and Statistical Signal Analysis
- Image Processing and Computer Vision
- Image Sequence Analysis
- Machine Learning and Pattern Recognition
- Deep Learning and Artificial Intelligence
- Robust Estimation and Bayesian Methods
- Data Science and Big Data Analytics
- Advanced Image Processing
- Medical Image Processing
- Remote Sensing and Image Analysis
- Programming and Software Engineering (Python, C++, MATLAB)
- Robotics and Intelligent Systems
- Engineering Project Management and Innovation

## Qualification and Academic Level

- French State-Accredited Engineering Degree (Diplôme d'Ingénieur)
- Master of Science in Engineering (MSc Eng. equivalent)
- Five-year higher education qualification (Bac+5)
- Equivalent to Level 7 of the European Qualifications Framework (EQF)
- Specialized engineering qualification in Images, Signals and Data Science
- Designed to prepare graduates for engineering and R&D positions in computer vision, signal processing, artificial intelligence, data science and advanced technological innovation.

## Skills Acquired

- **Signal & Image Processing:** Design and implementation of advanced signal and image processing algorithms, feature extraction, image analysis and computer vision.
- **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning:** Statistical learning, pattern recognition, deep learning and data-driven algorithm development.
- **Data Science & Statistical Analysis:** Data processing, mathematical modelling, statistical inference and robust estimation.
- **Software & Algorithm Development:** Engineering software solutions using Python, C++, MATLAB and associated scientific computing tools.
- **Applied Research & Innovation:** Translating scientific and mathematical concepts into practical engineering solutions.
- **Engineering & Project Management:** Technical project development, multidisciplinary collaboration and management of complex engineering challenges.
