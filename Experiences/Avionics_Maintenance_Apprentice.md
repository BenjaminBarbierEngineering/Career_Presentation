# Avionics Maintenance Apprentice

**Period:** 09/2018 - 08/2019  
**Company:** Sabena Technics  
**Location:** Aéroport de Nîmes Grande Provence Méditerranée, Grande Provence Méditerranée, 30800 Saint-Gilles, France

## Context

Within an aerospace maintenance environment, I was involved in the diagnosis, troubleshooting, and verification of onboard avionics systems. This experience enabled me to develop a strong understanding of the safety, quality, and rigor requirements that are essential in the aerospace industry.

## Responsibilities and achievements

- Participated in preventive and corrective maintenance activities on avionics systems.
- Applied aerospace maintenance standards and procedures for the preparation and execution of technical interventions.
- Developed and implemented test plans to verify the proper operation of avionics equipment.
- Provided technical support to maintenance teams working directly on aircraft.

## Results

- Performed maintenance activities on KC-135 aerial refueling aircraft operated by the Air Force, DC-10 tanker aircraft, and CL-415 firefighting aircraft (Canadair).

## Technical environment

- **Avionics maintenance:** Troubleshooting, test plans, aerospace technical documentation.
- **Onboard systems:** Aircraft instrumentation, avionics equipment.
- **Standards and procedures:** Aerospace maintenance procedures, aviation standards and regulations.
- **Reliability and diagnostics:** Failure analysis and fault investigation.
