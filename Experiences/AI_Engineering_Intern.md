# AI Engineer Intern – Facility energy consumption optimization using Reinforcement Learning

**Period:** 05/2022 - 08/2022  
**Company:** Vinci Facilities Alsace  
**Location:** 10 Rue du Chêne, 67800 Hoenheim, France

## Context

At Vinci Facilities, a major player in building technical and energy management, I contributed to the development of an artificial intelligence solution aimed at optimizing heating system control. The objective was to reduce building energy consumption through the intelligent adjustment of indoor temperature setpoints.

## Responsibilities and achievements

- Designed and developed an AI-powered decision support system for heat pump control.
- Developed Reinforcement Learning algorithms to automatically optimize temperature setpoints.
- Modeled the thermal behavior of buildings and their equipment using a digital twin based on sensor data.
- Simulated and evaluated various energy management and control strategies.

## Results

- Limited key outcomes, and the solution was not further reused by Vinci after the internship.
- Attempted to create a startup following the internship as part of a student entrepreneur initiative.

## Technical environment

- **Languages and development:** Python (TensorFlow, PyTorch).
- **Facility management:** Building Energy Management Systems (BEMS), heat pumps, HVAC systems.
