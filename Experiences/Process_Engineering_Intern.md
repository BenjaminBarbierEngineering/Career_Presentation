# Process Engineering Intern - Production Process Improvement 

**Period:** 06/2021 - 09/2021  
**Company:** Chromalloy Holland B.V.  
**Location:** Siriusstraat 55, 5015 BT Tilburg, Netherlands

## Context

At Chromalloy, a leading aerospace maintenance, repair, and manufacturing company, I was responsible for an industrial process improvement project focused on the production of Pratt & Whitney PW2000 High-Pressure Turbine (HPT) Stage 2 Air Seal components. The objective was to increase manufacturing efficiency, reduce machining costs, and improve production reliability while complying with stringent aerospace quality standards (AS9100 and EASA requirements).

## Responsibilities and achievements

- Led a production process optimization project from feasibility study to industrial implementation planning.
- Analyzed existing machining workflows and identified bottlenecks on CNC and Wire EDM operations.
- Designed and developed a new manufacturing strategy by consolidating multiple machining operations into a single automated process.
- Performed technical and economic assessments to validate process improvements and return on investment.
- Designed a dedicated Wire EDM fixture using Siemens NX, including tolerance analysis, clamping systems, positioning features, and assembly validation.
- Created detailed 3D CAD models and manufacturing assemblies to support tooling development.
- Developed CAM programs and optimized CNC machining strategies using Siemens NX Manufacturing.
- Created a custom postprocessor for an Agie Charmilles Wire EDM machine using NX Post Builder and TCL programming.
- Defined machining parameters, cutter compensation strategies, and EDM cutting sequences.
- Conducted machine motion simulations and manufacturing feasibility testing.
- Prepared production documentation, work instructions, and implementation plans for industrial deployment.

## Results

- Identified and validated a new manufacturing method projected to save approximately €12,000 annually.
- Reduced annual operator workload by 135 hours.
- Reduced annual machine utilization by 270 hours.
- Eliminated recurring Wire EDM production issues related to fixturing and wire-threading failures.
- Improved process robustness, automation level, and manufacturing repeatability.
- Delivered the complete technical solution, including tooling design, CAM programming, cost analysis, and implementation roadmap.
