# Intensive Preparatory Program for Engineering School Admission – Engineering Sciences (CPGE-ATS)

**Period:** 09/2019 - 08/2020  
**University / School:** Lycée Joliot-Curie  
**Location:** 144 Boulevard de Vitré, 35700 Rennes, France

## Program Overview

A demanding, one-year post-secondary preparatory program designed for students with a technical background (BTS, DUT or equivalent qualifications) seeking admission to leading French engineering schools (Grandes Écoles).

The program provided intensive multidisciplinary training in advanced mathematics, physics, computer science and industrial engineering. It aimed to strengthen scientific foundations, develop rigorous analytical and problem-solving skills, and prepare students for highly competitive engineering school entrance examinations.

## Major Subjects Covered

- Advanced Mathematics and Applied Mathematics
- Physics and Chemistry
- Industrial Engineering and Engineering Sciences
- Mechanical Systems Analysis and Modeling
- Electrical Engineering Fundamentals
- Automation and Control Systems
- Computer Science and Scientific Computing
- Scientific Modeling, Simulation and Problem-Solving
- Technical Communication and Scientific Reporting

## Qualification and Academic Level

- One-year post-secondary preparatory program (CPGE-ATS – Adaptation Technicien Supérieur)
- Specialized track: Industrial Engineering (Ingénierie Industrielle)
- Designed as a bridging program between technical higher education (BTS/DUT) and engineering school education
- Intensive preparation for national competitive entrance examinations, including written and oral assessments
- Preparation for admission to a three-year engineering degree program

## Skills Acquired

- **Mathematical Reasoning:** Advanced mathematical reasoning and quantitative analysis.
- **Scientific Problem-Solving:** Physical modeling and scientific problem-solving.
- **Engineering Systems:** Engineering systems analysis and technical modeling.
- **System Knowledge:** Understanding of mechanical, electrical and automated systems.
- **Scientific Rigor:** Scientific rigor, critical thinking and independent learning.
- **Communication and Exams:** Technical communication and examination performance.

## Projects, Experience, and Achievements

Ranked 12th nationally in the competitive engineering school entrance examinations, demonstrating strong academic performance among candidates from ATS preparatory programs.
