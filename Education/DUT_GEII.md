# University Diploma in Technology (DUT) – Electrical Engineering and Industrial Computing (GEII)


**Period:** 09/2016 - 08/2018  
**University / School:** University Institute of Technology (IUT) of Nîmes, University of Montpellier  
**Location:** 8 Rue Jules Raimu, 30900 Nîmes, France

## Program Overview

This two-year multidisciplinary university program provided a comprehensive foundation in electrical engineering, electronics, industrial computing and automated systems.

The curriculum focused on the design, implementation, programming, testing and maintenance of electrical and electronic equipment, embedded systems and industrial automation solutions. It combined theoretical engineering principles with practical laboratory work, technical projects and hands-on applications.

The program aimed to develop the technical and professional skills required to analyze industrial requirements, design and integrate technological solutions, operate automated equipment and troubleshoot complex electrical and electronic systems. It also provided a solid academic foundation for further studies in engineering and applied sciences.

## Qualification and Academic Level

- French National University Diploma in Technology (*Diplôme Universitaire de Technologie – DUT*)
- Specialization: Electrical Engineering and Industrial Computing (*Génie Électrique et Informatique Industrielle – GEII*)
- Two-year higher education qualification (Bac+2)
- 120 European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS) credits
- Level 5 of the European Qualifications Framework (EQF)
- Nationally recognized university qualification awarded by the University of Montpellier
- Designed to prepare graduates for technical positions in electrical engineering, electronics, industrial automation and embedded systems, as well as further studies in engineering schools, professional bachelor's degrees and university programs.

## Skills Acquired

- **Electrical & Electronic Engineering:** Understanding of electrical circuits, analog and digital electronics, electrotechnics, power electronics and electrical energy conversion.
- **Software Development & Embedded Systems:** Programming in C, microcontroller development, embedded electronics and low-level software implementation.
- **Industrial Automation & Control:** Design, programming and implementation of automated systems, control architectures and industrial processes.
- **Industrial Computing & Networking:** Understanding of computer architectures, industrial communication protocols, network infrastructures and data exchange between equipment.
- **Signal Processing & Instrumentation:** Signal acquisition, measurement systems, sensor integration, instrumentation and signal transmission.
- **System Integration & Troubleshooting:** Installation, configuration, testing, commissioning, maintenance and fault diagnosis of electrical and electronic systems.
- **Engineering & Project Management:** Technical requirement analysis, engineering project development, collaborative problem-solving and technical documentation.

## Projects, Experience, and Achievements

Throughout the two-year program, students completed laboratory assignments and technical projects designed to apply engineering concepts to practical industrial challenges.

These activities involved:
- Development of software applications and programming projects in C.
- Design, implementation and testing of electronic circuits and embedded systems.
- Programming and configuration of automated systems and industrial control solutions.
- Implementation and testing of industrial communication networks.
- Integration of sensors, electronic equipment and control systems.
- Measurement, signal acquisition and analysis using laboratory instrumentation.
- Testing, troubleshooting and validation of complete electrical and electronic systems.
- Development of a home automation application using an ESP32 to control actuators, including motors and garage systems.
- Development in C and C++ of an STM32 embedded system that analyzes vehicle OBD data over the CAN bus to analyze the diesel particulate filter.

The home automation and embedded systems projects were developed using C and C++.

This program provided a broad and practical engineering foundation in electrical engineering, electronics, industrial computing, automation and embedded systems, preparing graduates for technical careers in industrial environments and further specialization in engineering disciplines.
